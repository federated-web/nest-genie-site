The Problem

You're Not Failing. You're Carrying Too Much.

Modern parenting involves an invisible, relentless management layer that researchers call cognitive labor, the mental load.

Modern parenting comes with an invisible workload researchers call cognitive labor. In addition to handling 79% of repetitive daily responsibilities, mothers also manage 71% of household mental labor: remembering appointments, tracking medications, coordinating schedules, planning meals, and anticipating countless family needs.

For many parents, that work starts before the day officially begins. By 6 AM, lunches need packing, calendars need organizing, and important details need attention. While businesses rely on teams, assistants, and systems to stay ahead of complex operations, the person managing a family is often expected to keep everything running from memory.

The tools available today weren't built for that reality. Calendar apps require constant manual input, and smart speakers only respond when asked. Neither actively helps manage the mental load of family life.

That gap is why we built NestGenie.

The Solution

A Chief of Staff Who Thinks Ahead. A Personal Assistant Who Gets It Done.

NestGenie combines two things no other tool offers together: the strategic thinking of a Chief of Staff who anticipates what your family needs, and the hands-on execution of a Personal Assistant who handles the details.

Your Personal Assistant

NestGenie doesn't just remind you — it handles it. Meal planning, grocery orders, finding activities, scheduling appointments, researching schools. Like a real Personal Assistant, you tell it what you need and it gets it done.

"Find a swim class for Ella on Saturdays" → researches local options, compares schedules and prices, presents the top three with one-tap enrollment.
Your Chief of Staff

A Chief of Staff thinks ahead so you don't have to. NestGenie anticipates what's coming, offers evidence-based parenting guidance, and connects the dots across your family's life. At 2 AM when your toddler won't sleep, it's there with research-backed advice tailored to your child.

"Marcus keeps waking up screaming" → personalized guidance based on pediatric sleep science, your child's age, and what you've already tried.
Your Parenting Guru

Every parent is guided by different voices — the researchers, authors, and influencers whose approach just clicks. Tell NestGenie who shapes your parenting philosophy, and we'll build a guidance engine aligned to the principles you already trust. Dr. Becky? Janet Lansbury? Big Little Feelings? Your guru, your rules. And you can always tweak it as your family evolves.

"We follow Janet Lansbury's RIE approach" → NestGenie's guidance reflects respectful parenting principles in every recommendation, from tantrums to transitions.
Your Milestone Tracker

NestGenie tracks your child's development against CDC milestone data — so you always know where they are and what to look for next. First words, first steps, first smile. But it's more than a checklist. NestGenie automatically builds a family diary along the way, capturing the moments that matter — first day of daycare, first time putting their head underwater at the pool — so you have a keepsake you'll treasure forever, not just a medical record.

"Ella took her first steps today!" → Logged as a milestone, saved to Ella's diary with the date, and NestGenie updates her development profile. You'll get a beautiful timeline you can look back on for years.
